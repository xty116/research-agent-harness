Resolve the organization, person or product identity first. For competitors compare versioned capabilities and dates. For talent use public authorship or official affiliation announcements, preserving namesake ambiguity. For suppliers separate advertised capacity, verified delivery and sample quality. Cite originals and mark unavailable fields. Research supports a decision; it does not establish hiring suitability or procurement approval.
