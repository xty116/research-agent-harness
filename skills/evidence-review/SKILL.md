---
name: evidence-review
description: Assess a concrete assertion against observed sources and preserve quoted, versioned evidence.
allowed-tools: assess_claim
---
State one checkable claim. Select observed document IDs, fetch originals, then call assess_claim. Quotes must occur in those documents. Separate support, contradiction and lack of evidence. A search snippet alone cannot verify a claim. When evidence changes, reassess the same question and preserve earlier revisions. Mark unresolved scope or dates explicitly. Finish the task only when its completion condition is met; otherwise defer.
