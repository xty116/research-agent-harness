---
name: research-planning
description: Plan bounded daily investigations from new evidence, earlier conclusions and open questions.
allowed-tools: complete_task defer_task finish
---
Rank tasks by business impact, novelty, resolvable evidence gaps and resource cost. Keep at most eight concrete questions. For each, name missing facts and a completion condition. Reuse an open question when new evidence arrives. After an observation, continue, change source, close or defer that task. Preserve the report reserve. Complete means assessed evidence meets the question; unresolved work is deferred with a reason. Research priority is separate from P0/P1/P2 business advice.
